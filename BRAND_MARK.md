# Green Home Games mark

Metaphor: **a green moss mound with a warm cream doorway slit** — the
green home after winter, a shelf of fan games. The mark is a hill you
walk into, not a beaver portrait and not a lodge-on-fire.
At 32px the read is **green dome + cream door**, not a face, not a
hut-with-flame, not logs, not a sword, not a slingshot.

Shape: **full-bleed square field** of night purple. Primary silhouette
is a **squat moss-green haystack** (cosine-power mound with ridge knolls,
not a circle, not a snow-capped stick-lodge). A **tall cream door slit**
is cut in the front (arched rounded-rect, warm). Tiny yellow ember on
the doorstep only — not a chimney flame. Optional two ear-bumps on the
mound (ears only — moss knolls on the ridge, no face). No snow hat, no
chimney, no sword, no logs, no sling-V, no moon.

Palette (4):
- Night purple `#3A2A6A`
- Moss green `#3D6B38` (this mark's unique read)
- Cream `#F4E6C3`
- Ember yellow `#F5C400`
- (supporting) Door timber `#5C3A1A`
